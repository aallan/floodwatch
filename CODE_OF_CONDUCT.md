# Code of conduct

Floodwatch is built in the open, and anyone can report a problem, suggest a change or contribute code. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else the project is discussed, is expected to treat everyone else with respect, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What's expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to open source.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Criticise the work rather than the person, and accept criticism of your own work in the same way.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those they affected, and learn from them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Insults, derogatory comments, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may ban anyone from the project, temporarily or permanently, for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Decisions will be explained where that's appropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **alasdair@babilim.co.uk**. Every report will be reviewed and investigated, and answered as the circumstances need. The reporter's privacy will be respected.

This code applies in every project space, and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.
